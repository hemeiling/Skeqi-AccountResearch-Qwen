## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

SKEQI (思客琦) is a high-tech enterprise specialising in the R&D, production and sale of intelligent equipment, providing customers with overall intelligent manufacturing solutions centred on intelligent equipment [1]. The company describes itself as the “industry-leading module/PACK complete-line integrator” and “new-energy industry leading module/PACK complete-line provider” [1]. It is deeply rooted in the new-energy intelligent equipment field, with products covering cell assembly, module forming, PACK encapsulation and battery recycling/disassembly in the battery manufacturing process [1][2].

Its core product line is module/PACK equipment, applicable to power-battery and energy-storage-battery fields [2]. It also provides other-industry intelligent equipment for automotive/parts, engineering machinery and special equipment, as well as industrial digital software and services [2]. Its intelligent logistics solutions serve new-energy, automotive manufacturing and 3C electronics industries [1].

The company’s differentiation comes from non-standard customised equipment that integrates robotics control, laser processing, inspection/measurement, precision transmission, machine vision, algorithm control, industrial big data, mobile internet, information management systems and AI [2]. It states that it has “high technical maturity, strong cost control ability, and excellent delivery assurance” [1]. It also has a full lifecycle management system for R&D, design, manufacturing, commissioning, delivery and after-sales [2]. Third-party reports say it has integrated more than 2,000 sets of automation equipment and entered the front ranks of the new-energy segment [9], and one report calls it a “hidden champion” in the lithium-battery equipment field [8].

Market-rank figures differ across sources: [1] only says “TOP” segment share with no number, [8] says “one or two” in shipments, [4] says “top 2”, [5] says “first”, and [7] says “front rank”. Because the exact rank is conflicting, no precise rank is asserted here.
