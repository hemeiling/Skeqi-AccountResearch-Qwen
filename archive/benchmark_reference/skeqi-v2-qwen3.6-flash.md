## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
SKEQI (Shanghai/Ningde Skeqi Intelligent Equipment Technology Co., Ltd.) is a professional high-tech enterprise engaged in the research, development, production, and sales of intelligent equipment [1], [2], [5], [6]. The company functions primarily as an industry-leading integrator for new energy module and PACK complete production lines [1], [7]. 

**Industries Served:** Primarily new energy (power & energy storage batteries) and automotive manufacturing/components [1], [2], [5], [7]. The company also serves construction machinery, special equipment, 3C electronics, semiconductors, and emerging sectors including humanoid intelligence [2], [5], [7]. Its inspection technologies additionally support aerospace and electronic semiconductor sectors [1].

**Primary Products/Solutions:** Products cover the full battery manufacturing workflow, including cell assembly, module formation, PACK encapsulation, and battery recycling/disassembly [1], [2], [5], [6]. Additional offerings include automation lines and workstations for other industries, industrial digital software/services (e.g., Qihang digital factory system), laser application tech, X-ray non-destructive testing, precision manufacturing, and intelligent logistics systems [1], [2], [9].

**Core Business:** Deeply rooted in new energy intelligent equipment, specifically focusing on non-standard, customized automated production lines that integrate robotics, laser processing, machine vision, algorithms, industrial big data, and AI [2], [8].

**Differentiation:** SKEQI differentiates itself through strong multi-disciplinary technology integration, full lifecycle project management, and cost/delivery optimization enabled by its proprietary "Qizhizao" industrial R&D platform [2], [8]. It is officially recognized as a national high-tech enterprise, "Little Giant," manufacturing single champion, and industry "hidden champion," with reported domestic market leadership in module/PACK lines [4], [5], [6], [7], [8]. The company emphasizes rapid response, efficient delivery, and technological innovation to support high-speed capacity expansion for clients [1], [2].

## 4. BRAND
**Stated Values:** “诚信 创新 责任 感恩” (Integrity, Innovation, Responsibility, Gratitude) [1]; “协同共进，质赢未来” (Collaborate for progress, win future with quality) [1].
**Mission & Positioning:** Positioned as an “行业领先的模组/PACK整线集成商” (industry-leading module/PACK complete line integrator) and “新能源行业领先的模组/PACK整线提供商” [1]. The company aims to enable traditional Chinese industrial enterprises to achieve Industry 4.0 standards and drive the digital transformation of the TWh-era battery sector [4], [9]. 
**Strategic Ethos:** Operates as an “工业母机” (industrial mother machine) provider focusing on domestic substitution, high customization, and full-chain smart manufacturing integration [2], [8]. Brand messaging emphasizes long-term technological iteration ("十五年技术跃迁"), rapid delivery capability, and ecosystem collaboration to build an advanced energy equipment manufacturing ecology [1], [2], [9].
