# SKEQI / 思客琦 — ground truth for scoring

Retrieved independently 2026-09-02 from the **official website**, not from any model.
`www.skeqi.com` is a JS-rendered SPA: the served HTML is a 1.35 KB shell and all content
lives in a CDN bundle
(`img.website.xin/pubsf/54142/54142912/cdn-static-pages/pages/pc/7065_zh-cn.html.Body.js`).
A plain crawler or search-snippet pipeline therefore sees **almost nothing** of the official site.
This is the single most important constraint on the whole exercise.
